# Study Area Geographic & Agro-Climatic Profile

This study focuses on two premier agricultural districts located in the fertile Godavari delta of Andhra Pradesh, India: **East Godavari** (model development) and **West Godavari** (model validation).

## 1. East Godavari District (Model Development)

- **Geographic Center**: $17^\circ 19' 16.5''\text{ N}, 82^\circ 02' 16.5''\text{ E}$
- **Total Area**: $\sim 14,266.85\,\text{km}^2$
- **Physiography**: Godavari deltaic plain with alluvial soils, rich black cotton soils, and coastal sandy alluvium.
- **Irrigation Network**: Fed primarily by the Godavari River via the Sir Arthur Cotton Barrage at Dowleswaram, ensuring continuous canal irrigation for intensive double-cropping (Kharif and Rabi).
- **Climate**: Tropical climate with mean temperatures ranging from $25^\circ\text{C}$ to $35^\circ\text{C}$ and average annual rainfall of $\sim 1,100\,\text{mm}$.
- **Dominant Crop**: Paddy (Oryza sativa), occupying over $166,000\,\text{hectares}$ in the Rabi season.

---

## 2. West Godavari District (Model Validation)

- **Geographic Center**: $16^\circ 55' 02.7''\text{ N}, 81^\circ 20' 23.79''\text{ E}$
- **Total Area Analyzed**: $\sim 2,178\,\text{km}^2$
- **Physiography**: Adjoining western bank of the Godavari delta, renowned as the "Granary of Andhra Pradesh" due to highest regional rice yields.
- **Soil & Water**: Deep deltaic alluvium with high moisture retention capacity and canal/borewell irrigation.
- **Role in Study**: Used as a completely independent geographical testbed to prove that the empirical equation developed in East Godavari can reliably estimate yield in similar agro-climatic zones without recalibration.

## 3. Paddy Phenological Calendar (Rabi Season)

| Month | Phenological Stage | Crop Characteristics | Remote Sensing Implications |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dec – Jan** | Sowing & Transplanting | Puddled fields, waterlogged surface | Low NDVI, high water absorption |
| **Jan – Feb** | Vegetative / Tillering | Canopy development, tillering | Rapid rise in NDVI |
| **Late Feb – Early Mar** | **Heading & Flowering** | **Peak canopy greenness & biomass** | **Peak NDVI ($\sim 0.3 - 0.6$), optimal for yield prediction** |
| **Late Mar – Apr** | Ripening & Harvesting | Grain filling, canopy yellowing/senescence | Declining NDVI |
